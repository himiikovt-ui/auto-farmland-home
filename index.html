Auto Farmland

Auto Farmland is a private, local-first tool used by the shop owner to create, review, organize, and publish original digital products to their own Etsy shop.

Privacy

Application credentials, generated assets, listing drafts, and shop data are stored locally. This information is not sold or shared with third parties.
